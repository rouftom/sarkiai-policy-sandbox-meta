GLOBAL BEST-PRACTICE REFERENCE (illustrative synthesis for the sandbox).

[Data protection & health] The WHO guidance on ethics and governance of AI for
health recommends: (1) a named accountable data controller for every health-AI
system; (2) mandatory breach notification with a defined maximum delay;
(3) human oversight of automated decisions affecting care.

[Incident coordination] Published AI-readiness guidance suggests a
single designated lead authority for cross-sector incidents, with documented
escalation paths and joint situation reports.

[Standards & auditability] Global practice favours machine-readable audit trails
with source attribution and reproducible reasoning for any automated
policy-relevant decision.

[Infrastructure] Recommendations include redundancy of network and energy for
critical health services and a national data-protection impact assessment before
deploying generative AI on patient data.
